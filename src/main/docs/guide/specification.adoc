=== Chapter 1, the language

The eval-expressions, the literal-expressions and the composite expressions of the section 1.2, the literals of
the section 1.3, the resolution rules of the sections 1.5 and 1.6, the full operator set with the precedence of
the section 1.16, the functions of the section 1.18, the variables of the section 1.19, the lambda expressions of
the section 1.20, the enums, the arrays and `java.util.Optional` of the sections 1.21 to 1.23, the static field
and method references and the constructor references of the section 1.24, and the type conversion rules of the
section 1.25.

=== Chapter 2, the operations on collection objects

The construction of sets, lists and maps of the section 2.2, and the 22 operations on collection objects of the
section 2.3, implemented by `ELStream` and `ELOptional`.

=== Notes

Functions are bound when the expression is created, as required by the section 1.18: the compiler binds them from
`@ELFunctions`, and the interpreter binds them from the `jakarta.el.FunctionMapper` of the context, so a later
change of the mapper does not affect an expression that already exists.

The `jakarta.el.VariableMapper` and the `jakarta.el.ImportHandler` of the context are consulted at evaluation
time, as described in the sections 1.19 and 1.24. `MapVariableMapper` is provided as a map backed implementation
of the former.
